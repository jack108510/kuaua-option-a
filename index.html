Grant's Picks

Local favorites to make the most of your Maui stay.

Beaches

Ulua & Wailea Beaches — The best swimming and snorkeling right nearby.

Wailea Beach Path — Scenic 4km walk from the Andaz to the Fairmont.

Keawakapu Beach Walk — Head north from the Andaz toward Kihei. Park at the Ulua public lot.

Shopping

Shops of Wailea — Upscale shopping, dining, and galleries.

Hawaiian Moons (Kihei) — Organic groceries and local produce.

Mana Foods (Paia, 45 min) — For the adventurous organic shopper.

Health

Minit Medical (next to Manoli's) — Walk-in urgent care.

Nightlife

Mulligan's on the Blue — Live music & dancing, Sundays 5–8pm.

Maui Brewing Co. — Local craft beers and great vibe.

Dining

Manoli's Pizza — Best pizza in Wailea, right in the neighborhood.

Fairmont Kea Lani — DUO (steak/seafood) and Fish Market (casual).

Four Seasons — Spago (Wolfgang Puck) and Ferraro's (Italian oceanfront).

Shops of Wailea — Tommy Bahamas happy hour, Aurum dinner, Pint & Cork pub food, Kush fine art, Lappert's ice cream.

Andaz Poolside Patio — Drinks & light bites. Time it right for a free luau glimpse!

Kihei Food Trucks — Cheap, delicious, local.

Activities

Wailea Golf — Gold and Emerald courses, world-class ocean views.

Snorkeling — Molokini Crater and Turtle Town boat tours.

Haleakalā Sunrise — A must-do. 90 min drive, arrive early.

Transport

Uber/Lyft — Available but can be slow at night in Wailea. Plan ahead for late returns.

Rental Car — Recommended for full island exploration.
